Is the direction attached to the right note?

The orange box is the direction read from the scan; the blue line is the note the scanner would put it on (grey numbered lines are other notes). Right: right note (or a note of the same chord/onset) and the above/below shown is right. Wrong above/below: right note, wrong side. Right measure: right measure but another note; acceptable. Wrong: another measure or staff, or no sensible note. Not a direction: the box is not a direction/dynamic at all. Keys: R right, P wrong above/below, M right measure, W wrong, N not a direction, ← back.
